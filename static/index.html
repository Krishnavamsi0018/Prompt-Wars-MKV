    About LifeBridge

    From messy words to clear action.

    In an emergency, people describe what is happening in fragments: panicked, mixed languages, missing details. LifeBridge turns that unstructured situation into verified, actionable information: how serious it looks, what to do now, who to call, and a message you can send.

    1. 01

      Describe

      Tell us what happened.

    2. 02

      Understand

      Gemini interprets your words, images and context.

    3. 03

      Verify

      Structured results are checked against what was actually reported.

    4. 04

      Act

      Get relevant safety steps and a message you can share.

    AI interprets. Code verifies. Safety rules constrain.

    Frequently asked questions

    Straight answers about what LifeBridge does, and what it doesn't.

    What can I describe?

    Any situation where someone's health or safety may be at risk: a fall, a road accident, someone not responding, a fire, violence. Use your own words, in any language, and include who is hurt, what you can see and where you are.

    Gemini can read many languages. LifeBridge's built-in danger-sign checks currently recognise English and common Hindi phrasings.

    Can I add photos?

    Yes. Up to 3 photos of the scene or of medical papers, as JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, each under 5 MB. Photos are resized in your browser before they are sent.

    Anything LifeBridge reads from a photo is marked "From photo - please confirm", because code cannot check it against your words.

    Why does LifeBridge ask for my location?

    Only if you press "Share my location". It is used to add a Google Maps link to your SOS message and a "hospitals near my location" link. Your location is not sent to Gemini.

    Does LifeBridge diagnose medical conditions?

    No. It describes what you reported and shows general safety steps chosen from a fixed, pre-written set. It never gives a diagnosis or medication doses. The steps are adapted from public first-aid guidance and have not been reviewed by a medical professional.

    Does LifeBridge call emergency services automatically?

    No. LifeBridge never contacts anyone for you. The call buttons open your phone's dialler, and you choose whether to send the SOS message.

    What happens if Gemini is unavailable?

    You still get a safety card. It is marked "Offline safety mode" and is built from LifeBridge's own danger-sign rules: a severity level, relevant safety steps, emergency numbers and an SOS message using your own words. The Call 112 button stays on screen throughout.

    What information does LifeBridge store?

    LifeBridge does not use accounts or a database. Information submitted for analysis is sent to the Gemini API. Server logs may contain technical request information such as IP addresses.
